Detailed Description

template<typename... Out>
class seastar::rpc::sink< Out >

The sending end of an rpc stream.

close() must be called, and its future waited for, before the last reference to a sink goes away, even if the stream has failed. The stream's connection is shut down once the source on this connection has been read to eof or error as well.
